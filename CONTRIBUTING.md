## Contributing

Contributions are welcome, especially from people who use yt_xarray in
their own work. If you've hit a bug, need a feature, or found the docs
confusing, we'd like to hear from you.

To keep maintenance manageable, please note:

- **Open an issue first.** Describe what you're trying to do with
  yt_xarray and what you'd like to change. Small fixes for problems you ran
  into yourself (typos, broken examples) are fine as a direct PR.
- **Unsolicited PRs without prior discussion may be closed without review.**
  This includes PRs that pick up an open issue without commenting on it
  first, and PRs with no apparent connection to using the package.
- **AI-generated PRs from new contributors are not allowed** and will be
  closed.
- **This project is not a good fit for coursework or contribution-count
  PRs.** Maintainer time is limited, and we prioritize changes that come
  from real use.

See [the contributing guide](https://yt-xarray.readthedocs.io/en/latest/contributing.html)
for development setup and workflow.
